Department of Dravyaguna
Plumeria rubra
Botanical Name :

  • Plumeria rubra L.

    • Family Name :

  • Apocynaceae

    • Local Name :

  • Champa

    • Vernacular Name :

  • Hindi – Gulachin

  • English – Red Plumeria

    • Synonyms :

  • Raktachampa

  • Raktachampaka


    • Habitat :

  • Native to tropical America and widely cultivated in tropical and subtropical regions, grows well in warm climates, gardens, temple grounds, and roadsides.


    • External Morphology :

  • Stem: Small to medium-sized deciduous tree with thick, succulent branches containing milky latex.

  • Leaves: Large, simple, alternate, oblong to elliptic, clustered near the ends of branches.

  • Flowers: Large, fragrant, usually pink, red, or reddish-white, arranged in terminal clusters.


    • Useful Parts :

  • Root


    • RasadiPanchaka :

  • Rasa – Tikta, Katu

  • Guna – Laghu, Tikshna

  • Vipaka - Katu

  • Virya - Ushna


    • Pharmalogical Action :

  • Vedanasthapana

  • Vranaropana

  • Krimighna


    • Therapeutic Administration:

  • Kushta (Skin Disorders): Latex and bark preparations are traditionally used externally.

  • Krimi (Worm Infestation): Root preparations are traditionally used.


    • Dose

  • Churna: 1–3 g

  • Kwatha: 30–50 ml

  • Svarasa: 5–10 ml


    • Formulation:

  • Raktachampaka Churna

  • Raktachampaka Kwatha
